# Top Enterprise Marketing Automation Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Campaign Orchestration, Customer Data Platforms & Cross-Channel Engagement*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Enterprise Marketing Automation**. These tools enable organizations to unify customer data, segment audiences, orchestrate multi-step campaigns across channels, and measure marketing performance at scale.

**Examples** include Salesforce Marketing Cloud Advanced Edition, Adobe Experience Cloud, Oracle Marketing Cloud Eloqua, Marketo Engage, Acoustic Campaign, Pegasystems Marketing, Bloomreach Engagement, Braze, Iterable, and Zeta Global (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom CDP infrastructure, and open marketing automation — ideal for marketing teams, developers, and organizations seeking transparent, data-sovereign marketing platforms.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Salesforce Marketing Cloud Advanced Edition](https://www.salesforce.com/products/marketing-cloud/overview/)**  
  Enterprise marketing automation with Email Studio, Journey Builder, and Data 360 for unified customer profiles. Features AI-powered personalization via Einstein and Agentforce for autonomous campaign optimization. SQL Query Activities enable data blending across Data Extensions and Data Views for segmentation, with a 30-minute timeout limit requiring optimization for large datasets .

- **[Adobe Experience Cloud](https://business.adobe.com/)**  
  Comprehensive marketing suite with Adobe Campaign, Experience Platform, and Real-Time CDP. Features cross-channel campaign orchestration, AI-driven personalization, and unified customer profiles.

- **[Oracle Marketing Cloud Eloqua](https://www.oracle.com/)**  
  Enterprise B2B marketing automation with sophisticated lead nurturing, email campaign management, and revenue attribution. Features AI-powered content recommendations.

- **[Marketo Engage (Adobe)](https://business.adobe.com/products/marketo/marketo-engage.html)**  
  Enterprise B2B marketing automation with lead scoring, account-based marketing, and revenue attribution. Features visual workflow builder and native Salesforce sync.

- **[Acoustic Campaign](https://acoustic.com/)**  
  Enterprise email marketing and automation platform with AI-powered content recommendations, deliverability tools, and omnichannel engagement.

- **[Pegasystems Marketing](https://www.pega.com/)**  
  AI-powered marketing automation with real-time decisioning, customer journey orchestration, and predictive analytics.

- **[Bloomreach Engagement](https://www.bloomreach.com/)**  
  Commerce experience platform with journey orchestration, real-time segmentation, and AI-powered personalization across web, email, and mobile.

- **[Braze](https://www.braze.com/)**  
  Cross-channel customer engagement platform with Canvas journey builder, real-time segmentation, and AI-powered send-time optimization.

- **[Iterable](https://iterable.com/)**  
  Cross-channel growth marketing platform with visual journey builder, real-time segmentation, and A/B testing. Strong developer tools including SDKs and API.

- **[Zeta Global](https://zetaglobal.com/)**  
  Data-driven marketing platform with AI-powered customer acquisition and retention. Features identity resolution and omnichannel activation.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[LEO CDP](https://github.com/trieu/leo-cdp-framework)**  
  Open-source AI-first Customer Data Platform (license varies) for building customizable, self-hosted, privacy-friendly CDP infrastructure. Features omnichannel data collection, real-time Customer 360 profiles, AI-based segmentation (RFM, CLV, churn prediction), behavioral tracking, predictive analytics, audience activation, and Agentic AI personalization. Built with Java, Python, ArangoDB, and Apache Airflow .

- **[Helio](https://github.com/achref-soua/helio)**  
  Open-source, AI-native growth platform (AGPL-3.0) unifying CDP, segmentation, cross-channel journeys (email/SMS/push/WhatsApp), and analytics on Temporal workflow engine. Features natural-language segments, predictive scoring, brand-voice content generation, and MCP server for AI agents. Self-hostable via Docker, Helm, or cloud-init. Sustains ~20,000 events/s at p95 81ms .

- **[Mautic](https://github.com/mautic/mautic)**  
  The world's largest open-source marketing automation platform (GPL-3.0). 100% community-owned with no commercial entity behind it. Features multi-channel campaigns (email, SMS, social, web tracking), intelligent automation, unlimited segmentation, A/B testing, landing pages, and full data sovereignty. Used by 40,000+ companies including DB bahn.business .

- **[MessageBirds](https://github.com/Mullassery/MessageBirds)**  
  Open-source, event-driven Customer Data & Engagement Platform built with Rust + TypeScript. Features identity resolution, real-time audiences, governance, durable journeys, and Web/iOS/Android SDKs. Includes edge ingestion gateway and audience activation via PyReverseETL .

- **[Tracardi](https://github.com/Tracardi/tracardi-api)**  
  API-first composable open-source CDP engine (MIT with Common Clause). Helps you build your own Customer Data Platform with total control. Features customer data collection, profile unification, real-time personalization, marketing segmentation, and integration with internal systems. Docker deployment .

- **[Laudspeaker](https://github.com/laudspeaker/laudspeaker)**  
  Open-source customer journey automation and Braze/Iterable alternative (AGPL-3.0). Features visual no-code journey builder, event-triggered cross-channel messages (email, SMS, Firebase push), time delays, user segments, and PostHog integration.

- **[Dittofeed](https://github.com/dittofeed/dittofeed)**  
  Developer-focused open-source customer engagement platform (MIT). Features drag-and-drop journey builder, segmentation interface, web-based template editor, git-based workflows, testing SDK, and OpenTelemetry integration. Sends email, SMS, push, WhatsApp, Slack .

- **[OpenEC](https://pypi.org/project/openec/)**  
  Open Source AI-Powered Ecommerce & Retail Analytics Platform (license varies). Features modular plugin architecture with provider-agnostic adapters for Shopify, Amazon SP-API, WooCommerce, and Google Analytics. Includes customer cohorts, LTV tracking, campaign performance, funnel conversion, and pricing analytics .

- **[Listmonk](https://github.com/knadh/listmonk)**  
  High-performance, self-hosted newsletter and mailing list manager (AGPL-3.0) with a modern dashboard. Built in Go with Docker support. Features subscriber management, campaign creation, templates, and bounce processing .

- **[Matomo](https://github.com/matomo-org/matomo)**  
  Open-source web analytics platform (GPL-3.0) with marketing attribution, funnel analysis, and campaign tracking. Features consent management, role-based access, audit logs, and 70+ plugins. Integrations with WordPress, Shopify, and major CMS platforms. Webhooks work with Zapier, Make.com, and n8n for automation .

- **[Meridian (Google)](https://github.com/google/meridian)**  
  Open-source Marketing Mix Model (MMM) using Bayesian causal inference. Estimates incremental impact of marketing channels, provides response curves, and optimizes budget allocation. Includes Google's MMM Data Platform for impressions and clicks .

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[RudderStack](https://github.com/rudderlabs/rudder-server)** — Open-source customer data pipeline (AGPL-3.0) for collecting and routing events .
- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)** — Lightweight open-source CRM (AGPL-3.0) with marketing automation extensions .
- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)** — Open-source CRM (AGPL-3.0) with workflow automation and marketing modules .
- **[OroCRM](https://github.com/oroinc/crm)** — Open-source CRM (OSL-3.0) with e-commerce features and marketing campaign analytics .
- **[MailifyFlow](https://github.com/199ocero/mailifyflow)** — Self-hosted email marketing platform built with Laravel, FilamentPHP, and Amazon SES .
- Community **n8n** workflows for marketing automation and campaign orchestration.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **LEO CDP** or **Helio** as the CDP and journey orchestration core with **Mautic** for broader marketing automation, **Tracardi** for API-first customer data modeling, **Listmonk** or **MailifyFlow** for email delivery, and **Matomo** for analytics — creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade marketing automation platform.
